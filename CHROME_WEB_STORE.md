## Name
Shop Analyzer - Competitor Price Tracker

## Short description (max ~132 characters)
Compare your Shopify/Woo prices to competitors, get margin-safe moves, and watch price changes.

## Detailed description

Shop Analyzer helps Shopify and WooCommerce store owners see whether they are overpriced—and what to change—without a heavy enterprise pricing suite.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open your store and click “Analyze this shop”
2. Add competitor store URLs (catalogs are scraped in background tabs)
3. Confirm matches or pair products manually when names differ
4. Read the Truth Moment competitive score
5. Choose a pricing strategy and optional margin floor
6. Export CSV fixes, or star products to watch for competitor changes
7. Optional AI recommendations (bring your own API key: Claude, Grok, OpenAI, Gemini, or DeepSeek)

FEATURES
• Works with Shopify, WooCommerce, and many schema.org storefronts
• Category focus to ignore merch noise
• Automatic and manual product matching
• Suggested prices with margin-floor protection
• Watchlist with manual checks; scheduled alerts on Pro
• Side panel stays open while you browse
• Free tier available; Pro unlocks higher limits and scheduled alerts

HONEST LIMITS
• Always review matches—names and sizes can differ across shops
• Some protected storefronts return limited data
• AI features use your own keys and are optional
• Scheduled watch checks require Pro and Chrome running

Privacy: catalog analysis and settings stay in your browser storage. See the Privacy Policy linked on this listing.
