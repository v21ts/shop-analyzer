Shop Analyzer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Shop Analyzer (“the Extension”) is a Chrome extension that helps store owners analyze product prices and compare them with competitor storefronts. This policy explains what data is processed and how.

1. Data we process

The Extension processes data on your device in order to provide its features:

  • Storefront data you choose to analyze — product titles, prices, and related catalog fields from your shop and competitor URLs you enter. This is fetched from public pages or public store APIs (for example Shopify products.json or WooCommerce Store API) and from the active browser tab when you click Analyze.
  • Settings and results — saved analyses, confirmed matches, watchlist items, pricing strategy preferences, optional cost/margin settings, and license keys, stored using Chrome’s extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your computer.
  • Optional AI API keys — if you use AI recommendations, you may save your own third-party API keys (for example Anthropic, xAI, OpenAI, Google, or DeepSeek). Keys remain in local extension storage and are sent only to the provider you select when you request recommendations.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account to use the Free tier.
  • We do not operate a mandatory backend that receives your product catalogs for the core analyze/match/watch features.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not inject advertising trackers into websites you visit.

3. Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting / tabs — to read product information from the shop page you analyze and to open competitor pages briefly for scraping when you add a competitor or run a watch check.
  • storage — to save your analysis results, settings, watchlist, and optional license/API keys on your device.
  • alarms — to schedule optional competitor price checks (Pro).
  • notifications — to notify you when a watched competitor price changes (Pro / when enabled).
  • sidePanel — to show the analyzer UI in Chrome’s side panel.
  • Host access — to fetch catalog data from storefronts you choose and, if you use AI, to call the API endpoints of the AI provider you selected.

4. Third-party services

If you enable AI recommendations, catalog summaries you generate are sent to the AI provider you choose under that provider’s terms and privacy policy. Payment and license delivery (if you purchase Pro) may be handled by a third-party payment processor; that processor’s policy applies to billing data.

5. Data retention

Data stored via chrome.storage.local remains until you clear it in the Extension, remove the Extension, or clear site/extension data in Chrome.

6. Children’s privacy

The Extension is intended for business and store operators, not for children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised accordingly.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher email listed on the Chrome Web Store listing for Shop Analyzer.
