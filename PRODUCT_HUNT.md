## First comment

Hey Product Hunt 👋

I built Shop Analyzer because proper competitor price tools often start at $49–$99/mo and feel heavy if you just need a clear answer: “Am I overpriced vs these stores?”

What it does:
• Analyzes Shopify / WooCommerce catalogs
• Scrapes competitors and helps you confirm matches (including manual pairing)
• Shows a plain-English competitive score
• Suggests prices with an optional margin floor
• Watchlist with manual or scheduled competitor checks

Free tier is usable. Pro is $14/mo or $99/yr (lifetime optional for early supporters). AI is bring-your-own-key so costs stay transparent.

Would love feedback from anyone running a store — especially on matching quality and watch alerts.

Thanks for looking!
